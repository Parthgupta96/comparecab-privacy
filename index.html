Privacy Policy

Compare Cab for Android · Effective 21 August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Compare Cab app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Parth Gupta (collectively, the "Application"). Parth Gupta is referred to below as the "Service Provider".

How the Application works

Cab companies do not publish their fares through a public interface. To compare prices, the Application opens each cab app you have selected, one after another, and reads the fares shown on screen. It does this using Android's Accessibility Service, which you must turn on yourself.

What the Accessibility Service can and cannot see. While a comparison is running, the service reads on-screen text from the cab apps you selected, and only those apps. It is restricted at the operating system level to Uber, Ola, InDrive and Rapido, and cannot read any other app on your device.

It reads only what is needed to identify ride options and prices. It does not read, store or transmit passwords, messages, payment details or personal account information. The fares it collects stay on your device and are not sent to the Service Provider or to anyone else.

The service runs only during a comparison that you start, and stops when the comparison finishes. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

What stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone and is not visible to the Service Provider:

  • The fares and ride options read from the cab apps
  • Your saved and recent locations
  • Cached place and address lookups, which the Application stores to reduce repeat requests
  • Your app settings and preferences

Uninstalling the Application deletes all of this.

What the Application does not collect

The Application has no sign-in and no user accounts. It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details or contacts.

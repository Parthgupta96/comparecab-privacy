Privacy Policy

Compare Cab for Android · Effective 15 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Compare Cab app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by DailyTech (collectively, the "Application"). DailyTech is referred to below as the "Service Provider" and can be reached at app.dailytech@gmail.com.

The Application is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of the cab or grocery services it compares.

How the Application works

Cab companies and grocery stores do not publish their prices through a public interface. To compare them, you sign in to each service on its own website, inside the Application. The Application then opens those websites in the background, on your behalf, and reads the prices they show you.

The services it works with are Uber, Ola and Rapido for cabs, and Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Amazon Now and Flipkart Minutes for groceries. Grocery comparison may be switched on for only some users while it is being rolled out.

Signing in. You sign in directly on the service's own website. The Application never sees, stores or sends your password or one-time code. The resulting session (the website's cookies and storage) is kept only on your device and is never sent to the Service Provider. You can remove it at any time with Log out in the Application, or by uninstalling.

Nothing is booked or bought. The Application never books a ride, places an order or makes a payment.

Grocery carts. To compare your cart, the Application reads the items in your carts on the stores you have signed in to. If you agree when the Application asks, it gets each store's real bill, delivery and other fees included, by adding your items to that store's cart, reading the bill, and then putting the cart back as it was. Nothing is ordered or paid for.

Your location and route. To quote a fare or a delivery, your pickup point, destination or delivery location is sent to the service whose website is open, the same as when you use that website yourself. Your device location is shared only with that website, and only when you have allowed the Application to use your location.

What stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone and is not visible to the Service Provider:

  • Your sign-in sessions with the cab and grocery services
  • The fares, ride options, grocery prices and comparison results the Application reads
  • Your saved and recent locations, and the commute suggestions the Application learns from your searches
  • Cached place and address lookups, which the Application stores to reduce repeat requests
  • Your app settings and preferences
  • A record of the last fare comparison, used for troubleshooting, with anything that looks like a password or token blanked out

Uninstalling the Application deletes all of this.

Reports you choose to send

If you tap "Prices look wrong?", the Application prepares a report of the last fare comparison and opens Android's share menu. The report goes only where you choose to send it. Anything that looks like a password or token is blanked out before it is shared.

What the Application does not collect

The Service Provider runs no user accounts. The Application does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details or contacts. Your sign-ins are with the cab and grocery services themselves, under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and tracking technologies

The websites of the services you sign in to set their own cookies inside the Application, which stay on your device. The Application and the third-party SDKs listed below may use device identifiers and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics and advertising. Where applicable law requires it, the Service Provider will obtain your consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Advertising

The Application shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, which uses your Advertising ID to select and measure them. You can limit this at any time on your device:

  • Android 12 and later: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • Earlier versions: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
